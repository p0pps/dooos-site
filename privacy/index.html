Dooos

Privacy Policy

Effective August 31, 2026

What Dooos stores

Dooos stores the information you put into the app: household lists, items, notes, comments, member profiles, and activity history. It also stores identifiers needed to associate household members with their Apple Accounts and to keep shared records connected.

Where it is stored

Data is stored on your devices using Apple Core Data. When iCloud is available, Apple CloudKit stores and syncs the data and manages household sharing. Dooos does not operate a separate account or data server.

Apple's handling of iCloud data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Sharing within a household

A household uses one iCloud share. Members invited to that share can receive household data according to their iCloud permissions and the app's visibility settings.

“Just me” is an organising control, not a separate security boundary. The household owner and members granted full access may be able to see those lists.

What we do not do

  • No advertising.
  • No analytics or tracking SDK.
  • No sale of personal information.
  • No developer-operated account system.

Retention, deletion, and leaving

Household records remain in the owner's iCloud data until they are deleted. Deletions sync to other participating devices. The household owner can remove members and delete shared data; a participant can leave the household in Dooos Settings. You can export a local backup before deleting data.

Because Dooos does not operate its own account server, it cannot independently retrieve or delete data from your iCloud account. Use the controls in the app and your Apple Account settings to manage that data.

Questions

For privacy questions or support, use the Dooos support page. Do not include household content, Apple Account credentials, or invite links in a public issue.
