Dooos is published by Offsite Productions LLC.

Getting started

The household owner creates the household in Dooos and invites other people through Apple's iCloud sharing sheet. Every participant uses the Apple Account already signed into their device; Dooos does not have a separate username or password.

Joining a household

Installing Dooos and accepting a household invitation are separate steps. Install the app first, then open the iCloud invitation from the owner. Confirm that iCloud Drive is enabled and that the invitation was sent to the Apple Account used on the device.

Backups and deletion

Cloud sync is not a backup. Use the export option in Dooos Settings before destructive changes. The household owner controls shared membership and destructive household actions; participants can leave the household from Settings.

Contact support

Open a GitHub issue to report a problem, request help, or suggest an improvement. This support channel is public.

Do not post names, list contents, Apple Account information, credentials, screenshots containing personal data, or iCloud invitation links.
